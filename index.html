  • Rule 4: Exact change or digital transaction receipt — cash desks frequently turn students away without change.

Standard College Department Guide & Counters

🏛️

Student Section / Registrar Office

Handles Bonafide, Leaving certificates, Transcripts, Migration certificates, and official stamp endorsements.

Low-Wait Tip: Token counters often close at 12:30 PM for noon processing. Visit between 10:15 AM – 11:30 AM.
💳

Accounts / Fee Counter

Handles Fee receipts, Challan endorsements, No-Dues clearance, scholarship cheque disbursement, and refund requests.

Low-Wait Tip: Massive lines on the last 3 days of any installment deadline. Always verify if online portal / ERP fee payment is active.
📝

Exam Cell / Controller of Examinations

Hall ticket re-issues, grade sheet corrections, re-evaluation applications, backlog clearance verification.

Low-Wait Tip: Bring written endorsement from your Department Mentor/HoD before approaching exam in-charge.
📚

Central Library Desk

Book returns, overdue fine clearance, book bank scheme, Library No-Dues signature.

Low-Wait Tip: Can easily be batched with accounts visit. Digital book clearance takes under 3 minutes if books are already returned.
🏠

Hostel Warden Office

Room allotment, mess rebate slips, vacating inspection, gate pass approvals.

Low-Wait Tip: Wardens are usually available for signatures in designated morning (8:30-10 AM) or evening (5-6:30 PM) windows.
👨‍🏫

Department HoD / Mentor Cabin

Academic approvals, leave applications, elective add/drop forms, event NOC.

Low-Wait Tip: Always secure Mentor/HoD sign first before visiting central administration counters.
